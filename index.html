Lease Manager — Help & Training
Complete reference guide for the SPIG Lease Manager database. Built and maintained by the Facilities Manager.
📋 Overview — What Is This App?▶

The SPIG Lease Manager is a centralized database for tracking all commercial property leases across Service Pros Installation Group and its subsidiary brands — PF&T and ERX. It currently tracks ~75 workroom locations across the United States.

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and connects to a SharePoint list for live data storage. All changes made through the app (adding, editing leases) are saved directly to SharePoint in real time. The app is accessible to Directors, ROMs, AIMs, and C-Level leadership for read access, with Facilities Management controlling all edits.

💡 Data is read from SharePoint each time the app loads. If you just made a change, refresh the page to see it reflected.
Three Companies Tracked
SPIGService Pros Installation Group — the primary company. Most locations belong to SPIG.
PF&TPreferred Flooring & Tile — subsidiary brand. Location numbers are prefixed with PFT.
ERXERX subsidiary brand. Location numbers are prefixed with ERX.

📊 Dashboard▶

The Dashboard is the home screen and provides an at-a-glance summary of the entire portfolio.

Stat Cards (Top Row)
LocationsTotal number of active lease locations currently in the system.
Monthly Base RentSum of base rent across all active locations for the current month.
Total RentBase rent plus all OpEx charges (CAM, property tax, insurance, utilities, other).
Expiring ≤90 DaysCount of leases expiring within the next 90 days. Shown in red when urgent.
Avg Rent/SFAverage base rent per square foot across all active locations with square footage data.
Total SFTotal combined square footage across all active locations.
Active Alerts Panel

Lists leases that need attention right now — expiring soon or approaching their renewal notice deadline. Color coded: Red = within 30 days (or expired), Amber = 31–90 days. Sorted most urgent first.

Upcoming Renewals Panel

Shows active leases with an expiration date between 91 and 180 days away — outside the immediate alert window but within the next 6 months. These are leases where the renewal conversation with leadership should be starting. Sorted by soonest expiring first. When a lease crosses into the 90-day window it moves to Active Alerts automatically.

Soonest Expiring Panel

A quick-reference list of the next leases to expire, sorted by expiration date.

🗂️ Lease Data Fields — Full Glossary▶
Location Information
Location #The WR (workroom) number assigned to the location. PFT locations are prefixed PFT-, ERX locations are prefixed ERX-.
Location NameThe common name for the location (e.g. "Nashville", "Charlotte").
StatusActive, Inactive, or Month-to-Month.
Address / City / State / ZipFull physical address of the leased property.
Square FootageTotal leased square footage of the space.
CompanyAuto-determined from the location number prefix (SPIG, PF&T, or ERX).
Lease Dates
Lease Start DateThe original date the lease began.
Current Term StartStart date of the current lease term (may differ from original if renewed).
Current Term LengthLength of the current lease term in months.
Expiration DateThe date the current lease term expires.
Renewal Notice DeadlineThe date by which notice must be given to renew. Missing this date can result in automatic renewal or loss of the space.
Alert MonthAuto-calculated as the 20th of the month prior to the Renewal Notice Deadline. Used to trigger reminders.
Renewal TermsAny renewal options negotiated in the lease (e.g. "2-year option at same rate").
Financial — Rent
Base RentMonthly base rent amount due to the landlord.
Payment TypeHow rent is paid — ACH (auto-debit), Check, Bank Draft, or Ramp (escalating).
Rent Increase DateThe date the next scheduled rent increase takes effect.
New Base RentThe new base rent amount after the scheduled increase.
Financial — Operating Expenses (OpEx)

OpEx charges are additional monthly costs beyond base rent, billed by the landlord. The total CAM/OpEx column in the table is the sum of all items below.

Property TaxMonthly property tax pass-through from landlord.
CAMCommon Area Maintenance — shared costs for building upkeep, parking, landscaping, etc.
InsuranceLandlord's building insurance pass-through.
UtilitiesUtility charges billed through the landlord.
Other OpExAny other miscellaneous operating expense charges.
OpEx Increase DateDate when OpEx charges are scheduled to change.
People
DirectorThe Director responsible for this region/location.
ROMRegional Operations Manager for this location.
AIMArea Installation Manager for this location.
LandlordName of the landlord entity or individual.
Landlord ContactPrimary contact person at the landlord or property management company.
Broker InfoReal estate broker involved in the lease, if applicable.
Notes
NotesGeneral notes about the lease — history, special terms, issues, negotiations.
Rent Review NotesNotes specific to the monthly rent review process for this location.
Check Lease if RenewedCheckbox flag to indicate this lease needs to be pulled and reviewed if renewed.

✏️ Adding & Editing Leases▶
Adding a New Lease

Click + Add Lease in the top right corner of the app. Fill in all available fields and click Save. The record is saved directly to SharePoint and will appear in all views immediately.

Editing an Existing Lease

Navigate to All Leases (or any filtered view), find the location, and click the Edit button on that row. Make your changes in the modal and click Save. Changes are written to SharePoint in real time.

Required Fields

Location Name and Expiration Date are the minimum required fields. All other fields are optional but should be completed as thoroughly as possible for the app to be most useful.

💡 Only Facilities Management should add or edit lease records. All edits are saved to the shared SharePoint database and are visible to everyone immediately.
🔔 Alerts & Expiration Tracking▶

The app tracks three key dates per lease that cascade in urgency. Think of them as three stages of the same renewal event.

The Three Dates
Alert Month (Internal)Auto-calculated as the 20th of the month prior to the Renewal Notice Deadline. Stored on the lease record for reference — visible in the lease table and edit modal.
Renewal Notice DeadlineThe date by which notice must be given to the landlord. When within 90 days, the lease moves to Active Alerts. Missing this date can mean losing your renewal right or triggering an automatic renewal at unfavorable terms.
Lease ExpirationThe date the current lease term ends. Alerts fire when within 90 days of expiration.
Alert Colors
RedWithin 30 days of a deadline, or already expired. Requires immediate action.
Amber31–90 days from a deadline. Action needed — negotiation or decision should be in progress.
BlueExpiration is 91–180 days away. Shown on Dashboard "Upcoming Renewals" only — not on the Alerts page.
Where Alerts Appear
Alerts PageRed and Amber only. One card per location, all active alerts grouped inside it. Sorted most urgent first. No limit — all active alerts are shown.
Dashboard — Active AlertsSame as the Alerts page. All active red and amber alerts shown — no cap.
Dashboard — Upcoming RenewalsLeases expiring 91–180 days from today. Outside the immediate alert window but within the next 6 months — begin the leadership conversation about renewing or vacating.
💡 The red badge on the Alerts sidebar item shows the total count of active red and amber alerts across all locations.
💰 Monthly Rent Review Workflow▶

The Rent Review is the Facilities Manager's complete monthly process to verify all rent payments, apply any increases, reconcile payments against NetSuite, and communicate totals to leadership. Run around the 20th of each month in preparation for 1st-of-month payments. This workflow covers SPIG locations only.

The 10 Steps
1
Lease Expirations App
The app automatically pulls all active SPIG leases that are expired, expiring within the current review month, or expiring within 90 days. Leases approaching their renewal notice deadline are also flagged. Review each flagged location and confirm no lease has lapsed without action. Any leases requiring renewal negotiation should already be in progress. Use the Edit button to open and update a lease record directly from this step.
2
Rent & CAM Increases Due App
The app shows all active SPIG leases with a base rent or CAM/OpEx increase effective during the selected review month — split into Base Rent Increases and CAM/OpEx Increases. Click Apply to update the database with the new amount. The Apply modal also lets you record the next scheduled increase date and amount at the same time. If "Check Lease if Renewed" is flagged, a warning appears reminding you to pull the lease document and verify renewal terms before applying. Any increases applied here should also be updated in NetSuite's memorized transactions.
3
Special Payment Notes App
The app displays all active SPIG leases that have a Rent Review Note in the database. Review each note before payments go out — this is where you catch non-standard situations such as CAM reconciliation charges, credits, one-time charges, disputed amounts, prepaid periods, or anything else that affects what should be paid this month. Use the Edit button to update a note.
4
Closed / Inactive Locations App
The app shows leases that have recently become inactive. Cross-reference this list against NetSuite to confirm no payments are going out for closed or vacated locations. This is a safeguard against billing errors where payments continue after a location has closed or moved out.
5
New & Recently Moved Locations App
The app flags locations added to the database within the last 90 days, or locations with rent review notes containing keywords such as: prepaid, free rent, first month, starts, commenced, new lease, moved, or relocation. Early-lease situations frequently have non-standard payment schedules — free months, prepaid periods, or ramp structures. Verify the correct amount is being paid in NetSuite for each flagged location.
6
NetSuite True-Up NetSuite + App
In NetSuite:
  1. Open the saved search "Rent Review Search"
  2. Export the results as a CSV
In the App:
  1. Upload the CSV to the Step 6 panel
  2. The app compares each payment line 1-to-1 against the database using WR# and landlord name
  3. Locations with multiple vendors under the same WR# (e.g. Waynesboro Office/Warehouse, Nashville sub-leases) are matched by landlord name
  4. Ramp payment leases are excluded entirely
  5. Results: ✓ Match — amounts agree  |  ✕ Mismatch — amounts differ, investigate  |  ⚠ Not in Database — in NetSuite but not in app  |  — Not in NetSuite — in app but missing from report
Resolve all mismatches before moving forward.
⚡ NetSuite-Only Steps — Done Between Step 6 and Step 7
Mark Bank Drafts Paid
  1. Reference the Bank Draft vendor list from Step 7 in the app
  2. In NetSuite, open each Bank Draft vendor
  3. Click Make Payment
  4. Set the date to the 1st of the month you are paying rent for
  5. Select the rent bills from the list
  6. Go to the AFN Classic tab and select "Do Not Process With AvidXchange"
  7. Click Save
  8. Repeat for each Bank Draft vendor
Update Document Numbers
  1. In NetSuite, open the saved search "MCT Open Bills for Rent Review"
  2. Export as CSV
  3. Open the CSV and remove all non-rent bills — CAM reconciliation charges, water bills, utility bills, repair bills, and any other non-rent landlord charges
  4. Save the cleaned file as CSV
  5. In NetSuite, go to Setup → Import/Export → Saved CSV Imports
  6. Find and open #108 — Rent Payment
  7. Upload the cleaned CSV and click Run
Pay Rent Bills
  1. In NetSuite, go to Vendors → Accounts Payable → Pay Bills
  2. Set Start Date and End Date to the 1st of the month you are paying rent for
    Note: If paying CAM reconciliations entered on a date prior to the 1st, adjust the start date back far enough to capture those bills
  3. Set Type: All
  4. Set Vendor Category: Leaseholders
  5. Select each leaseholder bill to be paid
  6. Click Save
  7. A new page loads — click Refresh multiple times until all bills show as paid
  8. The resulting Processed Bill Payments screen is the source for Step 8 — do not close it yet
7
Bank Drafts App
The app shows all active SPIG leases on automatic bank draft — the same vendors you just paid in NetSuite above. For each one, verify the payment cleared and toggle the checkbox to ✓ Done. Status saves by month so you can return if needed. This step is a verification checkpoint only — no payments are issued from the app.
8
Leaseholder Reconciliation App + Excel
First Time Only — Seed Prior Month:

Upload the most recent Leaseholder Reconciliation Excel file. The app reads the Amount column and stores those values as Prior Month amounts. After the first run, amounts carry forward automatically.

Every Month:
  1. On the NetSuite Processed Bill Payments screen from the Pay Rent Bills step above, select from the Name column header down through the last vendor row
  2. Copy the selection
  3. In the app, paste into the paste area using Paste Special → Text
  4. Click Build Reconciliation

The app builds a table showing each vendor's Name, Status, Check #, Current Amount, Prior Amount, and Difference. Increases are highlighted amber, decreases are red. A summary shows total vendor count, current month total, prior month total, and net change.

  1. Click Download Excel to generate the Leaseholder Reconciliation file in the exact original format. Open in Excel — formulas recalculate automatically.

Current month amounts are saved automatically as next month's Prior Amounts. No manual copy/paste needed between months.

9
Email — Rent Ready to Process App + Outlook
To: Matt Thomas, Mary White  |  CC: Pam Jefferies  |  Subject: Rent is ready to be processed
The app pre-drafts the email body with the current month filled in. Click Copy Email Body and paste into Outlook. Attach the Leaseholder Reconciliation Excel from Step 8 before sending.
10
Email — Monthly Rent Summary App + Outlook
To: James Pace (JJ), Pam Jefferies  |  CC: Matt Thomas, Mary White  |  Subject: Monthly Rent - [Month Year]
The app pre-fills the current and prior month totals from the reconciliation. Click Generate Narrative to draft the explanation — the app uses vendor change data, matched location names, and rent review notes to build the narrative. If an Anthropic API key is configured in Settings, Claude AI writes the draft; otherwise a structured template is generated. Edit the narrative as needed to explain credits, CAM reconciliations, or special circumstances. Click Copy Full Email to copy the complete assembled email including greeting, narrative, dollar totals, and sign-off. Paste into Outlook and send.
💡 Navigate to Rent Review in the sidebar to run this process interactively with the app's built-in workflow tool.
📥 Exporting Data▶

Click Export CSV in the top right corner to download a full export of all lease data as a CSV file. This file can be opened in Excel and includes all fields — location info, financial data, dates, staff assignments, and notes.

The export always reflects the current live data from SharePoint at the time of export.

💡 The CSV export is useful for sharing data with leadership, running custom analysis in Excel, or creating a backup snapshot of all lease records.
👥 Staff Roster▶

The Staff Roster stores the names and emails of all Directors, ROMs (Regional Operations Managers), and AIMs (Area Installation Managers). This roster powers the Director, ROM, and AIM dropdown filters throughout the app.

Roles
DirectorSenior leadership responsible for a group of regions.
ROMRegional Operations Manager — oversees multiple workroom locations within a region.
AIMArea Installation Manager — day-to-day operational contact for one or more locations.
💡 The staff roster is stored in your browser's local storage. If you switch computers or clear your browser data, you will need to re-enter the roster. It is not stored in SharePoint.
