Lease Manager — Help & Training
Complete reference guide for the SPIG Lease Manager database. Built and maintained by the Facilities Manager.
📋 Overview — What Is This App?▶

The SPIG Lease Manager is a centralized database for tracking all commercial property leases across Service Pros Installation Group and its subsidiary brands — PF&T and ERX. It currently tracks ~75 workroom locations across the United States.

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and connects to a SharePoint list for live data storage. All changes made through the app (adding, editing leases) are saved directly to SharePoint in real time. The app is accessible to Directors, ROMs, AIMs, and C-Level leadership for read access, with Facilities Management controlling all edits.

💡 Data is read from SharePoint each time the app loads. If you just made a change, refresh the page to see it reflected.
Three Companies Tracked
SPIGService Pros Installation Group — the primary company. Most locations belong to SPIG.
PF&TPreferred Flooring & Tile — subsidiary brand. Location numbers are prefixed with PFT.
ERXERX subsidiary brand. Location numbers are prefixed with ERX.

🕐 Snapshot▶

Snapshot answers "how many locations were active on a given date (or during a date range) in the past?" It uses each lease's Lease Start Date and Expiry Date to reconstruct historical occupancy — no separate history log is kept, so accuracy depends on those two fields being correct on every record.

How it determines "active"
Active status todayCounted as occupied from Lease Start Date through today (ongoing).
Inactive statusCounted as occupied from Lease Start Date through Expiry Date only.
Single date vs. range

Pick an "As of" date for a point-in-time count. Check "Use range" to add a second date — this counts any location that was active at any point during that window, not necessarily the entire time.

Output

Shows a summary count, a mini-map with pins for the matching locations, and a data table below. Locations that moved to a new address are handled automatically — each lease record is evaluated on its own dates, so an old query date correctly shows the old address.

Exporting

Click ⬇ Export CSV to download the current table results as a CSV file, named with the selected date.

💡 If a historical count looks off, check Lease Start Date and Expiry Date on the affected records first — an incomplete or incorrect date is the most common cause, not a bug in the calculation.
✏️ Adding & Editing Leases▶
Adding a New Lease

Click + Add Lease in the top right corner of the app. Fill in all available fields and click Save. The record is saved directly to SharePoint and will appear in all views immediately.

Editing an Existing Lease

Navigate to All Leases (or any filtered view), find the location, and click the Edit button on that row. Make your changes in the modal and click Save. Changes are written to SharePoint in real time.

Required Fields

Location Name and Expiration Date are the minimum required fields. All other fields are optional but should be completed as thoroughly as possible for the app to be most useful.

💡 Only Facilities Management should add or edit lease records. All edits are saved to the shared SharePoint database and are visible to everyone immediately.
🔔 Alerts & Expiration Tracking▶

The app tracks three key dates per lease. Two drive active alerts; one drives the Dashboard Upcoming Renewals panel.

The Three Dates
Alert Month (Internal)Auto-calculated as the 20th of the month prior to the Renewal Notice Deadline. Stored on the lease record for reference — visible in the lease table and edit modal.
Renewal Notice DeadlineThe date by which notice must be given to the landlord. When within 90 days, the lease moves to Active Alerts. Missing this date can mean losing your renewal right or triggering an automatic renewal at unfavorable terms.
Lease ExpirationThe date the current lease term ends. Alerts fire when within 90 days of expiration.
Alert Colors
RedWithin 30 days of a deadline, or already expired. Requires immediate action.
Amber31–90 days from a deadline. Action needed — negotiation or decision should be in progress.
BlueExpiration is 91–180 days away. Shown on Dashboard "Upcoming Renewals" only — not on the Alerts page.
Where Alerts Appear
Alerts PageRed and Amber only. One card per location, all active alerts grouped inside it. Sorted most urgent first. No limit — all active alerts are shown.
Dashboard — Active AlertsSame as the Alerts page. All active red and amber alerts shown — no cap.
Dashboard — Upcoming RenewalsLeases expiring 91–180 days from today. Outside the immediate alert window but within the next 6 months — begin the leadership conversation about renewing or vacating.
💡 The red badge on the Alerts sidebar item shows the total count of active red and amber alerts across all locations.
🌪 Weather Safety▶

The Weather Safety tab gives anyone at a location — not just Facilities — a fast way to check current conditions, see any active National Weather Service alerts, find the site's shelter area and emergency contacts, and pull up the correct response steps for the type of weather happening. Select a location and a weather type from the dropdowns at the top to populate the page.

Current Weather & Active Alerts

Pulled live from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) based on the selected location's geocoded address — no API key or paid service required. Shows today's forecast conditions (temperature, sky, wind, precipitation chance) and any currently active NWS alerts for that address (tornado warnings, severe thunderstorm warnings, flood warnings, etc.), including the alert type and expiration time.

💡 If a location shows "Could not determine coordinates," it likely hasn't been geocoded yet — visit Map View once to trigger geocoding for that address, then return here.
Site Safety Info
Shelter AreaThe designated interior, away-from-exterior-wall location for that specific building. Facilities Management enters this — ROMs should confirm accuracy for their sites.
Hazard NotesBuilding-specific risks — roll-up doors, skylights, low-lying areas, large-span roofing, etc.
Local ManagerName and phone of the on-site point of contact.
Director / ROM / Landlord-PM ContactPulled automatically from the existing lease record for that location — not re-entered here.
Nearest HospitalFor reference in an emergency.

Only Facilities Management can edit these fields (the Edit button only appears for the app owner) — all other users have read-only access via the location dropdown, same permission model as lease data.

Response Protocol

Selecting a weather type shows the numbered response steps for that hazard — tornado, severe thunderstorm, hurricane, flash flood, winter storm, extreme heat, high wind, or power outage. This content is fixed reference material (not per-location) and reflects SPIG's standard inclement weather protocol.

Prep Checklists (Hurricane, Winter Storm, Extreme Heat)

These three advance-notice hazard types also show a step-by-step prep checklist below the response protocol, broken into timing stages (e.g., 72 hours out, 48 hours out, day-of, post-storm for hurricanes). A "Download PDF" link at the top of the checklist opens a printable version of the same content for posting at a site or attaching to an email.

💡 The PDFs live in the docs/ folder of the GitHub repo (docs/hurricane-prep-checklist.pdf, docs/winter-storm-prep-checklist.pdf, docs/extreme-heat-prep-checklist.pdf). If a download link 404s, the PDF hasn't been uploaded to that folder yet — see Claude for regenerating them if content changes.

💰 Monthly Rent Review Workflow▶

The Rent Review is the Facilities Manager's complete monthly process to verify all rent payments, apply any increases, reconcile payments against NetSuite, and communicate totals to leadership. Run around the 20th of each month in preparation for 1st-of-month payments. This workflow covers SPIG locations only.

💡 Review Month Dropdown: Always select the month rent is due — not the current calendar month. If today is June 20, select July, because July 1 is when rent is due. Everything in the workflow — expirations, increases, emails, and the Excel — will reference the selected month.
The 10 Steps
1
Lease Expirations App
Shows all active SPIG leases expiring on or before the last day of the selected review month. This catches any lease expiring in the current or prior months that hasn't been actioned yet — there should rarely be anything here, but it serves as a safety net. Expired leases (already past today) are flagged red and require immediate attention. Leases expiring by end of the review month are flagged amber. Use the Edit button to update a lease directly from this step.
2
Rent & CAM Increases Due App
Shows all active SPIG leases with a base rent or CAM/OpEx increase effective during the selected review month — split into Base Rent Increases and CAM/OpEx Increases. Click Apply to update the database with the new amount. The Apply modal also lets you record the next scheduled increase date and amount at the same time. If "Check Lease if Renewed" is flagged, a warning appears reminding you to pull the lease document and verify renewal terms before applying. Any increases applied here should also be updated in NetSuite's memorized transactions.
3
Special Payment Notes App
The app displays all active SPIG leases that have a Rent Review Note in the database. Review each note before payments go out — this is where you catch non-standard situations such as CAM reconciliation charges, credits, one-time charges, disputed amounts, prepaid periods, or anything else that affects what should be paid this month. Use the Edit button to update a note.
4
Closed / Inactive Locations App
The app shows leases that have recently become inactive. Cross-reference this list against NetSuite to confirm no payments are going out for closed or vacated locations. This is a safeguard against billing errors where payments continue after a location has closed or moved out.
5
New & Recently Moved Locations App
The app flags locations added to the database within the last 90 days, or locations with rent review notes containing keywords such as: prepaid, free rent, first month, starts, commenced, new lease, moved, or relocation. Early-lease situations frequently have non-standard payment schedules — free months, prepaid periods, or ramp structures. Verify the correct amount is being paid in NetSuite for each flagged location.
6
NetSuite True-Up NetSuite + App
In NetSuite:
  1. Open the saved search "Rent Review Search"
  2. Export the results as a CSV
In the App:
  1. Upload the CSV to the Step 6 panel
  2. The app compares each payment line 1-to-1 against the database using WR# and landlord name
  3. Locations with multiple vendors under the same WR# (e.g. Waynesboro Office/Warehouse, Nashville sub-leases) are matched by landlord name
  4. Ramp payment leases are excluded entirely
  5. Results: ✓ Match — amounts agree  |  ✕ Mismatch — amounts differ, investigate  |  ⚠ Not in Database — in NetSuite but not in app  |  — Not in NetSuite — in app but missing from report
Resolve all mismatches before moving forward.
⚡ NetSuite-Only Steps — Done Between Step 6 and Step 7
Mark Bank Drafts Paid
  1. Reference the Bank Draft vendor list from Step 7 in the app
  2. In NetSuite, open each Bank Draft vendor
  3. Click Make Payment
  4. Set the date to the 1st of the month you are paying rent for
  5. Select the rent bills from the list
  6. Go to the AFN Classic tab and select "Do Not Process With AvidXchange"
  7. Click Save
  8. Repeat for each Bank Draft vendor
Update Document Numbers
  1. In NetSuite, open the saved search "MCT Open Bills for Rent Review"
  2. Export as CSV
  3. Open the CSV and remove all non-rent bills — CAM reconciliation charges, water bills, utility bills, repair bills, and any other non-rent landlord charges
  4. Save the cleaned file as CSV
  5. In NetSuite, go to Setup → Import/Export → Saved CSV Imports
  6. Find and open #108 — Rent Payment
  7. Upload the cleaned CSV and click Run
Pay Rent Bills
  1. In NetSuite, go to Vendors → Accounts Payable → Pay Bills
  2. Set Start Date and End Date to the 1st of the month you are paying rent for
    Note: If paying CAM reconciliations entered on a date prior to the 1st, adjust the start date back far enough to capture those bills
  3. Set Type: All
  4. Set Vendor Category: Leaseholders
  5. Select each leaseholder bill to be paid
  6. Click Save
  7. A new page loads — click Refresh multiple times until all bills show as paid
  8. The resulting Processed Bill Payments screen is the source for Step 8 — do not close it yet
7
Bank Drafts App
The app shows all active SPIG leases on automatic bank draft — the same vendors you just paid in NetSuite above. For each one, verify the payment cleared and toggle the checkbox to ✓ Done. Status saves by month so you can return if needed. This step is a verification checkpoint only — no payments are issued from the app.
8
Leaseholder Reconciliation App + Excel
First Time Only — Seed Prior Month:

Upload the prior month's Leaseholder Reconciliation Excel file using the Seed Prior Month file picker. The app reads the Amount column and stores each vendor's prior payment as the baseline for comparison. After the first seed, prior amounts carry forward automatically each month — you only need to re-seed if you switch computers or clear your browser data.

Every Month:
  1. On the NetSuite Processed Bill Payments screen, select from the Name column header down through the last vendor row (stop before any Total row)
  2. Copy the selection
  3. In the app, paste into the paste area using Paste Special → Text
  4. Click Build Reconciliation

The app builds a table showing each vendor's Name, Status, Check #, Current Amount, Prior Amount, and Difference. Increases are highlighted amber, decreases are red. The prior month total includes all vendors from the previous month — including any locations that were active last month but have no payment this month (relocated, prepaid, etc.).

  1. Click Download Excel to generate the Leaseholder Reconciliation file. The app automatically updates: the report date (F1), the prior month label and total (D78/E78), and writes $0 for any vendor not in the current paste so formulas return 0 instead of errors.
  2. Open the downloaded Excel in Excel — formulas recalculate automatically on open.

Current month amounts save automatically as next month's Prior Amounts. No manual carry-forward needed.

9
Email — Rent Ready to Process App + Outlook
To: Matt Thomas, Mary White  |  CC: Pam Jefferies  |  Subject: Rent is ready to be processed
The app pre-drafts the email body using the selected review month. Click Copy Email Body and paste into Outlook. Attach the Leaseholder Reconciliation Excel from Step 8 before sending.
10
Email — Monthly Rent Summary App + Outlook
To: James Pace (JJ), Pam Jefferies  |  CC: Matt Thomas, Mary White  |  Subject: Monthly Rent - [Month Year]

After Step 8 builds the reconciliation, a Narrative Builder panel appears automatically with one card per significant change. Each card shows the location, vendor, and dollar change. The app pre-selects a suggested category for each change using two methods: scheduled increases from Step 2 are auto-categorized as Base Rent Increase or CAM Increase; and locations whose current payment matches their database base rent (while last month's was different) are flagged as Return to Normal. Review each card and confirm or change the category.

Each card has a text field pre-filled by AI with a suggested one-sentence explanation. Edit the text as needed. Toggle Skip for any change that doesn't need to appear in the email.

Click Generate Narrative to assemble the email body in the correct format:
  1. Opening sentence with month, direction, and net amount
  2. Prose paragraph — relocations, one-time charges/credits, decreases (from card explanation text)
  3. Base rent increases list (auto-formatted: Location - $Amount)
  4. CAM increases list
  5. Return to Normal section (auto intro sentence + list)
Review and edit the narrative before sending. Click Copy Full Email to copy the complete email — greeting, narrative, prior and current month totals, and sign-off. Paste into Outlook and send.
✓
Mark Month Complete App
After sending both emails, click Mark [Month] Complete at the bottom of the Rent Review page. This saves the month to the Rent Review History log, advances the review month dropdown to the next month, and clears the reconciliation state for the next run. Download the Excel and send the emails before marking complete — the reconciliation data clears when you do.

The Rent Review History collapsible section at the bottom of the page shows a running log of all completed months with current total, prior total, net change, and completion date.
💡 Navigate to Rent Review in the sidebar to run this process interactively with the app's built-in workflow tool.
